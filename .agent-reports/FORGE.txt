FORGE — FULL_SOLVENT direct-interface extraction repair
========================================================

Date: 2026-10-19
Scope: bounded synthetic tests only. No real 1BNA solve was run.

Root cause
----------
`Interface.make_net()` correctly constructed a dedicated A/B network while
retaining the full physical competitor universe. In wet mode that universe
included solvent, but `Network.refresh_solvent_provenance()` correctly marked
the dedicated interface network as `solvent_cells_complete=False` because its
group owns only the selected A/B generators.

The failure was raised by
`vorpy/src/results/shared_network.py:SharedNetworkIdentity.__post_init__`:
`FULL_SOLVENT` identities require `solvent_cells_complete is True`. The CLI
then passed the direct interface network's producer mode unchanged through
`VorPyBackend.make_interface_result_handoff()`. That identity gate belongs to
cell-dependent Results certification, not to direct physical A/B facet area or
direct atom-pair extraction. It must remain fail-closed for closed-cell,
buried-water, and complete solvent-cell metrics.

Correction
----------
`vorpy/workbench/services/vorpy_backend.py` now preserves the producer's
`FULL_SOLVENT` network and water competitors, but hands an incomplete direct
A/B result to Results under the explicit `SOLVENT_CONTEXT` partial contract.
The provenance records both `source_solvent_provenance` (the actual
FULL_SOLVENT producer) and `results_solvent_provenance` (direct-interface
scope). No SharedNetworkIdentity validation was relaxed and no competitor or
completeness flag was fabricated. Source-derived interfaces from an explicitly
requested full-system network retain the existing full-system identity path.

Geometry/evidence contract
--------------------------
The direct producer retains all constructed surfaces, edges, vertices and
generator membership. Existing extraction evidence separates:

* direct bicolor A/B facets and direct area;
* internal/supporting surfaces and total constructed area;
* supported versus unresolved direct facets and unresolved area;
* bounded/complete/supported edge and vertex closure;
* solvent-context evidence versus FULL_SOLVENT cell-dependent quantities.

Direct facet acceptance remains strict: finite nonnegative area, bounded and
complete local geometry, supported incidence, and the scheme-specific rules.
AW additionally requires complete participating AW cells for certified curved
face terms. POW and PRM use their local face/edge/vertex closure rules. The
direct geometry is exact for AW, POW and PRM in the bounded comparisons; PRM
typed Results handoff remains an explicit unsupported blocker.

Construction trace
------------------
`Network.build()` performs spatial indexing/sorting, `find_verts()`,
`connect()`, `build_edges()`, `build_surfaces()`, and then either `analyze()`
or `analyze_geometry_only()`. The selected `net.group` controls which cells
receive completeness and analysis values. Vertex discovery keeps a normal
vertex when it has at least one selected generator; interface discovery
requires both interface sides. Edge and surface incidence is then built from
the retained vertices. Solvent is retained in the SOLVENT_CONTEXT competitor
universe but is not a selected cell owner. No dual/alpha builder is called by
`Network.build()`; those are opt-in command analysis after primal geometry.

The completeness predicate is strict: every selected cell needs at least
three vertices, four edges, and three surfaces; every vertex must have the
expected three incident cell edges or three locally supported subset edges;
AW unresolved analytic vertices also invalidate the cell in full analysis.
There is no rule that removes a cell merely because it contacts solvent.

Correction
----------
The completeness code had a latent identity error: it used the incidental
`DataFrame.index` in the geometry-only cell map and full-analysis vertex
ownership check, although topology and `net.group` use the explicit generator
`balls['num']`. The correction uses `balls['num']` in both paths. This is
behavior-preserving for ordinary zero-based tables and prevents valid cells
from becoming incomplete after filtering, reload, or reindexing.

Incremental group completion milestone
=======================================

Date: 2026-10-19. This bounded prototype does not alter CLI, archive, export,
or EDTA construction paths. It accepts a solved source network and a requested
group, then either returns the existing exact `NetworkView`, materializes only
target-supported vertices plus exhaustively discovered bounded candidates, or
returns/raises an explicit normal-build-required result.

Reuse and dependency inventory
------------------------------

Vertices are reusable when their four-generator incidence, stable generator
IDs, location, radius, and completeness are valid and at least one generator
belongs to the requested group. Edges and surfaces are not copied blindly:
they are rebuilt from reused/candidate vertices, preserving incidence and
stable endpoint/face identities. Group cell measurements are compared after
reanalysis. Reuse requires matching scheme, full physical generator universe,
positions/radii, boundary configuration and IDs, solvent/context membership,
resolution, solver/build settings, and solver version. Source group membership
is deliberately excluded from the compatibility comparison so Group C can be
derived from a compatible A/B source.

Candidate validation enumerates all four-generator combinations only within a
bounded generator/combination budget and verifies every candidate against the
complete source competitor universe, including retained solvent/context
generators. AW uses both roots; PRM uses primitive distance; POW uses the
production squared-power bound. Incomplete source incidence is recorded as
evidence and never promoted to complete.

Limitations and ATLAS requirements
----------------------------------

The materialization path is intentionally bounded and requires an independent
clean target reference before it can be certified. Inputs over the search
budget, missing stable identities, incompatible dependency digests, incomplete
candidate search, or failed geometry comparison require an explicit normal
build. The prototype is not yet wired into checkpoint incremental completion.
ATLAS must persist stable generator/feature identities, source context and
solver digests, partial vertex/cell evidence, lineage, invalidated analysis
names, and completeness status atomically; stale or partial records must not
be loaded as complete geometry. VECTOR, PRISM, and archive serialization were
not modified.
