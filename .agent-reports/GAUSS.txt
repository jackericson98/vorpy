------------------

reference values. No 1BNA solve or arbitrary completeness exception was run.

FULL_SOLVENT semantics
----------------------
Solvent membership and solvent-cell completeness are separate evidence.

* MOLECULE_ONLY excludes competing explicit solvent generators.
* SOLVENT_CONTEXT retains physical water/ion competitors and records that
  solvent cells are not globally complete. It is sufficient for direct atom
  pair contacts, bounded supported physical A/B facets, direct physical area,
  and surrounding-water membership/context.
* FULL_SOLVENT retains the same competitors and additionally requires
  complete solvent-cell evidence. It is required for complete solvent-cell
  metrics, closed-cell volume/topology, buried-water groups, and alpha results
  whose acceptance depends on complete higher-dimensional cells. The ATLAS
  SharedNetworkIdentity fail-closed error “FULL_SOLVENT requires complete
  solvent cells” is correct for that identity; direct physical area must use a
  solvent-context/partial contract when this evidence is absent.

Feature-specific requirements
-----------------------------
Direct physical facet area requires a valid A/B bicolor mapping, finite area,
bounded and complete local surface geometry, and supported incidence. AW also
requires complete participating generator cells because its clipped curved
patch can be truncated by an incomplete cell. PRM/POW area does not inherit
that AW cell requirement, but still requires local face/edge/vertex closure.
Direct atom-pair contacts use pair geometry/contact criteria; complete solvent
cells are not required. Solvent presence alone never certifies buried water,
closed topology, or complete curvature.

Area accounting contract
------------------------
All values are in Å² and must be named independently:

* direct_interface_area: only constructed Group-1/Group-2 facets;
* internal_supporting_network_area: Group-1 internal, Group-2 internal, and
  supporting/unclassified facets, not direct contact;
* total_constructed_network_area: finite-area sum of every constructed network
  surface; it is never a direct-contact metric;
* known_supported_partial_area: finite area of the locally supported direct
  subset, with supported/total facet counts;
* unresolved_direct_area: finite area of omitted/incomplete direct facets when
  known; otherwise null, never zero-filled.

The export ledger now reports these fields from the existing surface classifier.
VERIFIED means every direct facet is supported and finite. PARTIAL means a
supported subset exists and other direct facets are unresolved. UNRESOLVED
means no certifiable direct subset exists. NOT_CALCULATED means extraction was
not attempted. In typed Results, VERIFIED maps to Status.CERTIFIED; PARTIAL,
UNRESOLVED, and NOT_CALCULATED retain their corresponding Status semantics.

Curvature/topology contract
---------------------------
NOT_CALCULATED means curvature/topology was not requested or not attempted;
all unavailable quantities are null. UNRESOLVED means it was attempted but
required incidence, integration, closure, or boundary terms could not be
proved. A measured zero is a finite zero with CERTIFIED or PARTIAL status, not
an unavailable value. Partial H/K retains finite supported terms plus
support_count/total_count; a complete total remains null/UNRESOLVED.

An open interface is not a closed surface: boundary edges are recorded, genus
and closed-surface totals are not inferred, and uncalculated boundary
Gaussian/mean terms remain unavailable. An open-boundary mean total may be
certified only under the explicit no-free-boundary-scalar-term convention and
only when all required face/seam terms are supported. Gaussian totals require
manifold/orientable incidence and the required Gauss–Bonnet terms.

Alpha acceptance
----------------
Pair-level alpha selection may be PARTIAL when pair births map but higher
simplex/cell births are unresolved. It is NOT_CALCULATED when skipped,
UNRESOLVED when attempted and blocked, and CERTIFIED only when all selected
births/mappings needed by the requested alpha result are supported. Alpha is a
separate derivative and never replaces direct physical facet area.

Acceptance labels
-----------------
VERIFIED direct geometry: authoritative solve/source identity; all direct
facets and required local closure supported; finite direct area; no unresolved
direct facets. PARTIAL: same evidence for a strict subset, with explicit
coverage and unresolved area. Complete local physical features require
bounded/complete/supported surface, edge, and vertex records. Complete AW
curvature additionally requires participating AW cells. Topology/curvature
and alpha remain UNRESOLVED or NOT_CALCULATED unless their own evidence passes.

Cell completeness audit
=======================
Status: SCIENTIFIC CRITERIA READY FOR FORGE / NEXUSA; ATLAS COORDINATION

Observed symptom and cause boundary
------------------------------------
The historical EDTA value of 32 is a producer ``balls.complete`` count under
one recorded solve, not a proof that all 32 three-dimensional Voronoi cells
are bounded, closed, or suitable for Gauss--Bonnet. The independent diagnostic
now keeps three facts separate: producer reconstruction evidence, AW
boundedness, and local incidence closure. A low count cannot be attributed to
solvent adjacency without per-cell evidence. No EDTA calculation was run for
this audit.

Formal completeness criteria
----------------------------
For a physical generator, computational reconstruction is complete for a
requested local metric only when the generator identity and all required
incident surfaces, edges, and vertices exist; every referenced index is valid;
the required generator cardinalities are correct; feature coordinates and
areas are finite; and the required incidence relations are supported.

This is not the same as a bounded or closed cell. With BoundaryMode.NONE,
finite-generator hull cells can have a proven non-zero AW recession direction.
They are ``UNBOUNDED`` even when their finite local faces are reconstructed.
They cannot support closed-cell volume, closed topology, or closed-surface
Gauss--Bonnet. A numerical shell/explicit boundary generator is an enclosure,
not a physical cell; contact with it does not invalidate an adjacent physical
cell when the local feature records are complete.

For closed-cell metrics, additionally require bounded geometry, every cell
edge incident to exactly two cell surfaces, valid vertex links, connected
manifold incidence, and orientability. The current per-cell audit exposes
missing faces, missing edges, missing vertex references, open edges, and bad
vertex links; it is necessary evidence, not by itself a global manifold proof.

Diagnostic classification table
-------------------------------
| Evidence | Classification | Scientific consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Proven recession cone | ``UNBOUNDED`` | Not a reconstruction failure; no closed-cell metric |
| Bounded cell with missing feature/incidence or open closure | ``INCOMPLETE_CONSTRUCTION`` | No complete local/closed metric |
| Boundedness solver unavailable or analytic branch unresolved | ``UNSUPPORTED_ANALYTIC_GEOMETRY`` | Boundedness/curvature unavailable, not zero |
| Closure and boundedness pass but producer flag is false | ``OVERLY_CONSERVATIVE`` | Recheck producer bookkeeping; do not silently promote |
| Producer flag true but bounded-cell audit finds defects | ``INCOMPLETE_CONSTRUCTION`` | Producer flag cannot override independent evidence |
| Producer flag true and local audit passes | ``COMPLETE`` | Complete only for the proven local scope; bounded/closed remains separate |

Solvent and boundary rules
--------------------------
Solvent is a competing generator, not an automatic invalidation. A physical
cell may touch solvent and remain locally complete. Solvent invalidates a
requested result only when a required solvent incident feature is absent or
when the result claims complete solvent-cell metrics without complete evidence
for every participating solvent generator. ``Network.refresh_solvent_provenance``
currently establishes mode and membership (all solvent indices in the group);
that field is not an independent per-cell closure proof and must not be read
as one. ``FULL_SOLVENT`` therefore remains fail-closed for complete solvent
metrics, while local SOLVENT_CONTEXT geometry may remain usable.

AW / POW / PRM distinction
--------------------------
Primary local incidence checks are scheme-neutral. AW additionally needs the
weighted recession/boundedness interpretation and complete participating AW
cells for certified curved AW face/edge/alpha results. POW and PRM can certify
a finite planar pair feature from its own supported local geometry without
inheriting the AW whole-cell gate; closed-cell volume/topology still requires
its own bounded closure evidence. Dual simplices and higher-order alpha births
are derivative support. They must not mutate or become hidden requirements of
the primary ``balls.complete`` predicate. The read-only dual interface test
locks this separation.

Bounded scientific acceptance tests
------------------------------------

without invalidation; stale producer-complete flags versus topology defects;

Full-system acceptance requires per-cell reason evidence, independent counts
of cells/features, explicit boundary and solvent membership, no unresolved
required incidence, and separate closed/manifold/orientable checks before
closed-cell curvature or Gauss--Bonnet is certified. Missing curvature remains
unavailable, never numeric zero.
