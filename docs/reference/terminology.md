# Terminology

**Ball** — spherical generator used by the decomposition. In molecular systems this commonly represents an atom with a center and radius.

**Cell** — spatial region assigned to one generator.

**Surface** — boundary separating neighboring cells.

**Edge** — geometric intersection between Voronoi surfaces.

**Vertex** — point at which Voronoi edges meet.

**Group** — user-defined subset of the system with its own selections, build settings, and exports.

**Interface** — shared Voronoi boundaries connecting two molecular groups.

**AW** — additively weighted Voronoi.

**POW** — power/Laguerre diagram.

**PRM** — primitive/unweighted Voronoi.

**Representative Surface Energy** — curvature-dependent reference bending metric derived from integrated squared mean curvature; not a calibrated molecular free energy.
