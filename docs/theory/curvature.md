# Curvature

VorPy can calculate local mean curvature, local Gaussian curvature, integrated mean curvature (`int_mean_curv`), integrated squared mean curvature (`int_mean_curv_sq`), and integrated Gaussian curvature (`int_gauss_curv`).

Curvature is calculated from a discrete triangulated representation rather than an analytic continuous surface, so results can depend on surface resolution, triangulation quality, local geometry, and numerical implementation.
