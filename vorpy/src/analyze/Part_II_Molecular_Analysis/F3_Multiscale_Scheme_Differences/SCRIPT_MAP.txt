INTENDED VORPY SCRIPT FOLDER
============================
vorpy/src/analyze/Part_II_Molecular_Analysis/F3_multiscale_differences/

F3_common.py
    Shared AW/Power matching, atom/residue/molecule aggregation, surface-area
    and contact definitions, and signed/absolute deviation functions.

F3A_multiscale_summary.py
    Main atom -> residue -> molecule comparison for Volume, Surface Area,
    Contacts.

F3B_atomic_groups.py
    Protein and nucleic-acid AW-vs-Power atom-value plots; refactor of
    F2C_All_Data_Sets_Clustered.py.

F3C_residue_groups.py
    Signed residue-type distributions; refactor of 3C_Residue_type_diffs.py.

F3D_geometry_examples.py
    Data-driven selection/export of representative atom and residue geometry.

F3E_solvent_exposure.py
    Buried/partial/exposed scheme-deviation analysis for atoms and residues.

F3_build_figure.py
    Final figure compositor only.
